# Atlas Steering Committee — Minutes, 2026-08-27

Present: programme sponsor (chair), finance director, operations director,
delivery lead, PMO lead. Apologies: IT director. Quorum met. These minutes were
circulated on 2026-08-28 and are the record of decisions taken.

## 1. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of 2026-07-30 were accepted without amendment.

## 2. Delivery report

The delivery lead reported Atlas as amber, driven by the finance workstream. The
committee noted that the August status report had already been circulated and
took the report as read. No questions were raised on the warehouse workstream.

## 3. Milestone M2

### 3.1 Discussion

The delivery lead set out that M2 is forecast two days late as a consequence of
41 reconciliation exceptions from the second migration rehearsal. The finance
director asked whether the exceptions represented a data quality problem or a
mapping problem. The delivery lead confirmed the latter: 31 of the 41 trace to
cost centres retired in the 2024 restructure and never mapped.

The operations director asked whether M3 float could be used to recover M2. The
chair confirmed the standing position of the committee that M3 float is reserved
for M3 and is not available to recover an earlier milestone.

### 3.2 Decision

**Decision SC-2026-08-01.** The committee deferred the decision between
accepting the M2 slip and funding a recovery rehearsal to the September meeting,
on the condition that the third migration rehearsal completes before then and
its exception count is reported. If the exception count is at or below the
tolerance of 10, the committee will accept the slip and take no further action.

Owner: delivery lead. Due: 2026-09-10.

## 4. Budget

### 4.1 Forecast overrun

The PMO lead reported a forecast at completion of 515,000 USD against an
approved 480,000 USD, an overrun of 35,000 USD or 7.3%. The finance director
noted that the delegated tolerance of the programme is 5% and that an overrun
above that requires a formal change request to the investment board, not a
steering committee decision.

### 4.2 Decision

**Decision SC-2026-08-02.** The committee instructed the PMO lead to prepare a
change request to the investment board for an additional 40,000 USD, covering
the forecast overrun and a 5,000 USD contingency. The change request is to be
submitted whether or not the September rehearsal succeeds, because the overrun
has already been incurred.

Owner: PMO lead. Due: 2026-09-15.

## 5. Vendor escalation

The committee discussed the failure of the second integration vendor to confirm
a payments test window after three written requests. The chair directed that the
matter move to contractual escalation under the master services agreement rather
than continue as correspondence.

**Decision SC-2026-08-03.** Escalate to the account director of the vendor in
writing, citing the service credit provision, with a five working day response
deadline.

Owner: Priya Raman. Due: 2026-09-03.

## 6. Any other business

The operations director asked when hypercare staffing would be confirmed. The
delivery lead undertook to bring a hypercare staffing plan to the September
meeting. No decision taken.
