# Orion CRM Migration — Delivery Status Report, September 2026

Reporting period: 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-31. Orion is a separate programme from
Atlas, sharing neither budget, steering committee, nor delivery team. It appears
in this corpus so that cross-project access control has something real to refuse.

## 1. Summary

Orion is **green**. All three milestones are on plan, the programme is 4% under
budget, and no risk above medium severity is open.

## 2. Milestones

Milestone O1, contact data consolidation, completed on 2026-06-12. Milestone O2,
pipeline migration, is due 2026-10-02 and is on track with three weeks of float.
Milestone O3, decommission of the legacy CRM, is due 2027-01-15.

## 3. Budget position

As of 2026-08-31 Orion has spent 118,400 USD of an approved 260,000 USD, with a
forecast at completion of 249,000 USD.

## 4. Risks

One risk is open: O-1, that the pipeline migration cannot be verified without a
production data extract that legal has not yet released. Severity medium, owned
by the Orion delivery lead, review date 2026-09-18.
